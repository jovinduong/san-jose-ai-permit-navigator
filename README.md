# AI Permit Navigator: AI for Small Business Permits
Client: City of San Jose, small business permit guidance
Team: Jovin Duong, Jose Garcia, Elias Duong, Ishaan Pabla, August-Oct 2026
My part: I tested the AI Permit Navigator prototype in Replit, identified usability issues in the user information section, and documented suggestions for improving the user experience.
## The problem
Small business owners in San José may struggle to understand which permits they need to start or operate their businesses. The permitting process can be confusing because requirements and procedures are not always easy to understand.
## What we built
- Developed an AI-powered prototype that helps small business owners identify potential permit requirements and generates a personalized action plan.
- Created a user interface where business owners can enter their information, select their business type, and view potential permit requirements, including an option to manually enter business types not listed in the dropdown menu.
- Included a human review process where city staff can review AI-generated permit guidance before determining whether the information is accurate and appropriate.
## What I would do next
One remaining area for improvement is testing the prototype with a wider variety of small business owners to identify potential usability issues. I would conduct additional user testing and use their feedback to improve the user information process and make the interface easier to navigate.
